# Design update — 3D models (13 October 2026)

The design kit of [stage 1](../2026-10-03-design/) is kept as published. This folder holds the updated 3D models only (kit version 74): the room model with the distribution board and the control cabinet modelled in detail, and separate models of the board, the cabinet, its door mechanism and a ladder over the radiator. The specification and the cable schedule have changed with these updates; the revised lists, drawings and workshop files will follow once they are settled.

All files are self-contained HTML (three.js embedded): download and open in a browser, no internet needed. Left mouse — rotate, right — pan, wheel — zoom, click — description of the element.

| Model | Open | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| Room | [3d-model.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/3d-model.html) | The whole room: power wiring, lighting, ventilation, sensors, control cabinet, air conditioner, cameras, bathroom with the cistern frame duct box, ladder over the radiator. Conduit follows the black ceiling band and turns 90° down to each point. The DB and the CC are now modelled inside the room; the cabinet door opens with a button. |
| Distribution board DB | [3d-db.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/3d-db.html) | EKF two-door enclosure 580×490×165 mounted upside down, meter at the bottom: entries, conduits, cables and cores to the terminals, circuit table. |
| Control cabinet CC | [3d-cc.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/3d-cc.html) | Welded cabinet 1000×1060×300, bottom at 0.76 m: devices on the mounting panel, cable glands in the top, cable routes, computer and monitor, UPS and batteries, PR200 + PRM. |
| CC body and door | [3d-cc-door.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/3d-cc-door.html) | The cabinet body for the workshop: frame on feet, cladding, guide rails, two glass panels that slide down on 2:1 wire ropes, 24 V linear actuator with 550 mm stroke. Dimensions and a description of every part. |
| Ladder over the radiator | [3d-ladder.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/3d-ladder.html) | Variant 5: welded frame of 40×40 and 40×20 tube with chequer-plate steps, standing in front of the radiator to reach the window. Parts, cut angles, welds, step-by-step assembly, parts list. |
| Ladder sketches | [ladder-sketches.html](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-13-design-update/ladder-sketches.html) | Six variants compared on climbing, radiator heat, look and simplicity; variant 5 chosen. |

Designators are those of stage 1: DB — distribution board, CC — control cabinet, JB1/JB2 — junction boxes, S1–S5 — sockets, SW1/SW2 — switches, LPU — leak protection unit, CAM1/CAM2 — cameras. Cabinet body parts: FR1–FR4 frame tubes, D1–D5 door-mechanism parts, BP back panel, MP mounting panel, SL/SR side panels, TP top, BT bottom. Ladder parts: L1.1 uprights, L1.2 wall arms, L2.1–L2.3 stringer sections, L3 steps, L4 base plates; welds W1–W7.
