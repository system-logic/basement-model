Ladder over the radiator

Six variants of a decorative structure in front of the radiator that you can stand on to open the window. Drawn to the dimensions of your wall: window recess, radiator, heating pipes, edge of the control cabinet and the air conditioner route. Variant 5 is chosen — refined: no top shelf and no bottom frame.
